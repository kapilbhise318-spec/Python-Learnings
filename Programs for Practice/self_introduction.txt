Here is a simple self-introduction for a Software Developer fresher 
that you can use in interviews:

Self Introduction:

"Good morning/afternoon, sir/madam.

My name is Kapil. I have completed my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) 
from Pune University and my Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) from Nanded University.

I have a strong interest in software development and programming. My primary programming
 language is Python, and I am familiar with technologies such as Flask, FastAPI, SQL,
  and Git. I have also learned basic concepts of Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming,
   APIs, and Machine Learning using Scikit-learn.

As a fresher, I have worked on personal projects such as an ATM Management System, Number
 Guessing Game, QR Code Generator, and web applications using Flask. These projects helped
  me improve my problem-solving skills and understanding of software development.

I am a quick learner, hardworking, and eager to contribute to a professional development
 team. I am looking for an opportunity where I can apply my technical skills, learn from 
 experienced professionals, and grow as a Software Developer.

Thank you."

Short Version (30 seconds)

"Hello, my name is Kapil. I have completed my MCA from Pune University and B.Sc.
 from Nanded University. 
I am passionate about software development and have skills in Python, Flask,
 FastAPI, SQL, and basic Machine Learning. 
I have built several personal projects to strengthen my programming skills.
 As a fresher, I am eager to learn, 
contribute to real-world projects, and grow my career as a Software Developer."



Important Tips
Speak confidently and clearly.
Mention 2–3 projects from your resume.
Focus on your skills and willingness to learn.
Keep the introduction between 1–2 minutes in interviews.
